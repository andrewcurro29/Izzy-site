My Story

Redirected, and grateful for it.

"Fitness has never been just about physique, skill, or the numbers on the scale. It's been the relationships a common goal can curate."

The stories shared before class. The personal questions asked afterwards. A goal being shared, set, then reached, or the unexpected exchanges to and from the parking lot. THAT is what fitness has become to me.

Along the way

  1. TamaracFitness finds me young. Childhood sports, then soccer at Tamarac High School, give me something to pour myself into.
  2. CollegeTwo ACL and meniscus tears end my college soccer career at 19. Recovery becomes the start of everything that follows.
  3. Portland, MaineFive years in the city I still call my second home. I find yoga at Portland Yoga Collective and start leading every kind of class I can: HIIT, yoga, Pilates, soccer clinics, personal training.
  4. OrangetheoryHead Coach at Orangetheory Niskayuna. I hit my 1,000th coached class in June 2025, and co-led a packed Flexibility & Mobility Workshop in January 2026.
  5. October 2025After missing the first marathon I ever trained for, I finish the Maine Marathon in Portland, a year after running the half on the same course.
  6. AlbanyEleven classes a week and one-on-one coaching at Best Fitness on Central Ave, and a Special Guest spot on NEWS10's Upstate Living.

"Through injuries, comebacks, reps, and miles, I've built more than just strength. I've built trust in myself."

Izzy, on Instagram

Nutrition coaching

Eat for the life you're training for

Nutrition coaching with me is offered through Best Fitness, Central Ave. No crash diets and no foods you're never allowed to eat again. Together we build simple habits that fuel your workouts, your recovery and your busy days, and that still work long after we're done.

Food first

Habits over rules

We start with what you already eat and change a few things at a time: protein at every meal, more color on the plate, a plan for busy weeknights.

Performance

Fuel for how you move

  • Fuel before you train or play
  • Refuel after workouts, games and long runs
  • Hydrate, hydrate, hydrate
  • Race-week and race-day fueling
Accountability

Someone in your corner

Regular check-ins to see what's working, adjust what isn't, and keep you consistent when life gets busy.

"She brings a vibe that keeps fitness and nutrition clients showing up."

How it works

  1. 1. Free intro chatTell me about your goals, your schedule and how you eat now. No judgment, just a starting point.
  2. 2. Your starting pointA few days of simple food notes (photos count) so we can see your real patterns.
  3. 3. Your planTwo or three habits to focus on first, built around your training, your work and the foods you actually like.
  4. 4. Check-insWe review, adjust and add the next habit, with check-ins scheduled through Best Fitness.
